Welcome to Why Matters-Challenge #1

This may seem unrelated to Tableau, but the first step in the Agentic Era is to understand how to properly provide the data.

Challenge: You have a 100-row CSV file containing critical metrics, and you need your AI agent to turn it into a Markdown table. You try to upload the file to the chat window, but it fails. Which door holds the correct plain-text workaround?

Door 1
In a new chat block, drag and drop the CSV file so it is the only thing in the chat block.
Door 2
Open the CSV file in a text editor or spreadsheet application and copy-paste the raw data into the chat window.
Door 3
Take a screenshot of the CSV and paste that into the chat window.

Bonus Question 1

What is the best way to tell AI this pasted text is specifically CSV code?

Bonus Question 2

When pasting a really large amount of data, the AI drops some of the data. Pick the door that holds the best workaround!
