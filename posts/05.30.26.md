*Published: 05.30.26*

Data is just a bunch of strings and integers until you capture the logic behind it. Whether it comes from a database, a flat file, or an API, the metadata is what makes it make sense.

### The IfThenWhy™ Field Explorer

The IfThenWhy Field Explorer is an open library of those metadata fields, designed to align with the core Logic RFC standard. It acts as a centralized baseline to map data elements directly to human intent and functional logic, rather than locking them away in platform-specific silos.

Check out the foundational fields below and submit your own feedback if you have ideas on how to improve them:

* **Business Context Fields:** Anchoring categorical tags and metric identities straight to the core business owner.
* **Logical Mappings:** Defining the underlying validation parameters and calculations without hardcoding database physical constraints.
